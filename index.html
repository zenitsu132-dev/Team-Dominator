// What is this?

Dominate the Storm.
Beat the Odds.

Team Dominator is a Roblox private server set in a storm-ravaged world. Two teams battle for control of the map while tornadoes, hail, and lightning bear down on both sides.

Earn XP for every chase, unlock new vehicles and roles, and climb from Rookie Spotter all the way to EF5 Elite.

Weather events are live and procedurally driven — no two storms are the same. Can you survive a direct hit?

// Tornado Scale

Know Your Storm

EF1 🌬️
EF1 — WEAK
65–110 MPH
Minor damage. Branches snapped, light debris. Great conditions for new players to get into the action.
EF2 🌩️
EF2 — STRONG
111–165 MPH
Significant damage. Cars overturned. Requires experienced players and good team coordination.
EF3 🌀
EF3 — SEVERE
136–165 MPH
Extreme damage. Walls collapse. Only veteran players attempt to dominate zones in these conditions.
EF5 ☠️
EF5 — VIOLENT
200+ MPH
Total destruction. Rare and terrifying. Only EF5 Elite rank players can survive close intercepts.
// Server Rules

Server Conduct

01

No Exploiting or Hacking

Any unauthorized scripts, speed hacks, or weather manipulation exploits result in a permanent ban.

PERMA BAN
02

Respect All Players

No slurs, harassment, or targeted griefing. Competitive trash talk is fine; bullying is not.

WARNING
03

Don't Block Storm Paths

Intentionally blocking other players from zones or griefing their team's progress is unsportsmanlike.

KICK
04

Obey Staff Decisions

Staff calls are final during active sessions. Disputes go in the ticket channel, not public chat.

KICK
05

Stay In Character (SIC)

This is a Team Dominator server. Keep chat focused and use team comms during active rounds.

WARNING
06

Have Fun & Stay Safe

It's a game. Chase hard, compete fairly, and enjoy the ride — even when the storm wins.
